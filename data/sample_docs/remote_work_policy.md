# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to work remotely up to three days per week. Managers may approve fully remote arrangements on a case-by-case basis for roles that do not require regular in-person collaboration.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time home office stipend of $500 for employees working remotely more than two days per week. Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps.

## Core Hours
Remote employees must be available and responsive between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone, regardless of which days they work remotely. Outside of core hours, employees may adjust their schedule as needed as long as they meet their deliverables.

## Security Requirements
Remote workers must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems and must not use public Wi-Fi networks without the VPN enabled. Company laptops must have disk encryption and automatic screen lock enabled at all times.

## International Remote Work
Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their country of employment must receive written approval from HR and Legal at least 30 days in advance, due to tax and labor law implications. Requests for international remote work longer than 30 consecutive days require a separate mobility agreement.
