# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One
IT will ship your laptop and equipment to arrive no later than three business days before your start date. You will receive an email with instructions to set up your company email and Slack account 48 hours before your start date.

## Week One
New employees complete mandatory compliance training covering data privacy, information security, and code of conduct within their first five business days. Your manager will schedule 1:1 meetings with your immediate team members during week one.

## 30-60-90 Day Plan
Employees work with their manager to define specific goals for the first 30, 60, and 90 days. The 30-day check-in focuses on ramping up and understanding team processes. The 60-day check-in evaluates early contributions. The 90-day check-in, which coincides with the end of the probationary period, is a formal performance conversation with HR involved.

## Benefits Enrollment
Health insurance, dental, and vision benefits must be elected within 30 days of the start date or the employee must wait until the next open enrollment period. 401(k) enrollment is available immediately, with the company matching 50% of contributions up to 6% of salary.

## Probationary Period
The standard probationary period is 90 days, during which either the employee or the company may end employment with two weeks notice. Remote work eligibility, as described in the Remote Work Policy, begins after the probationary period ends.
