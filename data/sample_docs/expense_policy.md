# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## General Guidelines
All business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the expense date. Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by a Vice President.

## Travel Expenses
Economy class airfare is standard for flights under 6 hours. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with manager approval. Hotel expenses should not exceed $250 per night in major metropolitan areas and $150 per night elsewhere, unless attending a conference at a specific venue.

## Meals
Employees traveling for business may expense up to $75 per day for meals, itemized by breakfast ($15), lunch ($20), and dinner ($40). Alcohol is reimbursable only when entertaining clients, up to $50 per event, and requires the client's name and company in the expense notes.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses require pre-approval from a director or above for amounts exceeding $200. All client entertainment expenses must list the attendees and business purpose.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company does not reimburse for personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, airline club memberships, or upgrades to first class under any circumstances.
